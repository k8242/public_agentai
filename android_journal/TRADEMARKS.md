## Guidelines for Forks & Redistributed Builds

If you fork, modify, or redistribute ThreadLog (for example, on Google Play, F-Droid, or another store):

1. **Retain MIT Attribution (Required)**: Keep the copyright notice and MIT permission notice (`LICENSE` and `NOTICE`) in your source repository and in any compiled binary distribution (for example, in your app's About / Open Source Licenses screen).
2. **Rebrand Unofficial Distributions**: Change the app display name (`app_name`), app icon, and `applicationId` so users can clearly distinguish your build from official ThreadLog releases.
3. **Truthful Attribution Is Encouraged**: You may truthfully state that your app is *"Based on ThreadLog by k8242"* or *"Forked from ThreadLog"* in your About screen, README, and store description, provided you do not imply official endorsement.
4. **Unmodified Personal Builds**: Building ThreadLog from source for personal use or contributing pull requests upstream does not require rebranding.
